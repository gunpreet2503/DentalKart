🔧 IF TESTS FAIL:

Most common issue: Selectors don't match your HTML structure

Fix in 2 steps:
  1. Run: ./inspect-selectors.sh
     (Opens browser, helps you find correct selectors)
  
  2. Update: src/test/java/com/dentalkart/pages/SearchPage.java
     (Change lines 15-20 with correct selectors)

See README.md "How Selectors Work" section for examples.
